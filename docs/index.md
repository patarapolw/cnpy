## About the vocabulary

The level lists are from the [ZhQuiz project](https://github.com/zhquiz/level). The dictionary excerpts are from [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict), published by MDBG under the [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Definitions are shown in dictionary order; numbered pinyin marks the tones.
